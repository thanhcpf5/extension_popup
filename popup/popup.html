About

This extension provides a step-by-step tutorial overlay for supported pages with Vietnamese text-to-speech support. You can enable/disable the tutorial, toggle voice narration, restart it, or view info here.

🔊 Text-to-Speech Features:
• Automatic Vietnamese voice narration
• Manual speech control with 🔊 button
• Contextual step descriptions
• Smart language detection
